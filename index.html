2 · Summary of Incident

Yolanda intensified into a super typhoon about 48 hours before landfall and struck the central Philippines on 8 November 2013 with devastating winds and a tsunami-like storm surge. It is recorded as the deadliest and costliest typhoon in Philippine history. Communities across Eastern Visayas, Western Visayas and Palawan suffered massive loss of life, destruction of homes, collapse of communications and power, and the loss of livelihoods.

5 · Timeline of Events

  1. ~6 NOV 2013Typhoon passes Micronesia as a typhoon; government begins preparations.
  2. 7 NOV 2013Enters PAR and is named Yolanda; Public Storm Warning Signal No. 4 issued along its path.
  3. 8 NOV 2013 — LANDFALLMakes landfall in Eastern Visayas; storm surge inundates coastal communities, Tacloban City devastated. Weakens in the evening after crossing the country.
  4. 9 NOV 2013Exits PAR toward Vietnam and southern China; storm signals lowered in some areas.
  5. 11 NOV 2013Early damage estimates reach as high as ₱650 billion (later official tally far lower).
  6. 13 NOV 2013Government reports 4,460 dead; international relief operations ramp up.
  7. LATEROfficial toll climbs to 6,300 dead; recovery and rehabilitation runs for years, with survivors protesting its pace.

8 · Recommendations

  1. Strengthen early warning and evacuation, especially for storm-surge-prone coasts.
  2. Build stronger, more resilient housing and critical infrastructure.
  3. Improve casualty tracking and transparency in aid release.
